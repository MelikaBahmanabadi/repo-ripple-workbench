01 — Our Story

Born in a small
Portland roastery,
built on ritual.

Ember & Oak began as a two-seat counter behind a friend's woodshop — one grinder, one lever machine, and a stubborn belief that coffee deserves time. A decade later, the room has grown but the ritual hasn't changed.

We roast in small batches every Tuesday, cup every batch blind, and pull every shot by hand. Our beans travel direct from farms we visit each harvest, and our pastry is laminated on marble slabs three steps from the bar. Come early, stay long.

03 — The Experience

From bean
to your hands.

  1. 01

    Sourcing & Roast

    Beans from 48 farm partners across nine countries, roasted in small batches each Tuesday and rested to peak flavour.

  2. 02

    The Perfect Extraction

    18 grams in, 36 grams out, 27 seconds. Every dose weighed, every shot timed, every crema judged by eye.

  3. 03

    Milk & Microfoam

    Local whole milk steamed to silk — glossy microfoam at exactly 62°C, poured the moment it's ready.

  4. 04

    Latte Art & Craft

    The final pour is where craft meets theatre — rosettas, tulips and hearts, drawn freehand in under four seconds.
