01 — Our Story

From a two-seat counter
to a home for ritual.

Ember & Oak began behind a friend's woodshop — one grinder, one lever machine, and a stubborn belief that coffee deserves time.

We roast every Tuesday, cup every batch blind and pull every shot by hand.
